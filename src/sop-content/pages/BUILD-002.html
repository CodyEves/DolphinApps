01Purpose

Nightly updates tell members who couldn't come what happened in a subsystem. It is the subsystem lead's job to make sure everyone knows what is happening in their subsystem. Team rule

02Format

Copy this and fill it in:

Date

day and date

Subsystem

for example Hopper Floor, Programming, Outreach

Members

everyone who worked on it (tag them)

Progress

what got done, specific enough to act on

Problems

what went wrong and what was tried

Next steps

what the next person should do first

Photos or video

whenever possible

NOTICE

"I don't want to go into too much detail" is not an update. Write enough that someone could pick up the work tomorrow without asking you.

03Where and when

  1. Post in #5199-nightly-updates or #9271-nightly-updates.
  2. Post before the next shop session. The same night is best.
  3. If the lead is absent, they make sure someone who was there posts it.
  4. Leads read every update for their subsystem.

Copy this SOP

Team 5199 and Team 9271 publish this manual so other teams can copy and adapt it. Change the names, numbers and blanks to fit your shop.
