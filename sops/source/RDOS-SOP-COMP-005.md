## At a glance
1. **Select:** drive team roles (see Roles)
2. **Practice:** drive practice (see Selection)
3. **Pre-match:** checklist every match (see Pre-match checklist)
4. **Debrief:** after every match (see After each match)

## Roles
| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| Driver | Drives the robot |
| Operator | Runs mechanisms |
| Drive coach | Calls strategy and watches the field |
| Human player | Handles game pieces at the station |
| Technician | Robot connection, battery and quick fixes at the field |

## Selection
1. Selection follows handbook section 11.3: skill, composure, practice time, coachability, safety, availability and game knowledge.
2. Names for each event are posted in the event channel before playoffs.
3. Read FIRST's drive coach guidance: [EDIT: link to the current FIRST drive coach document].

## Pre-match checklist
- [ ] Fresh battery, tested, strapped in
- [ ] Bumpers in the right color, secure
- [ ] Robot connects to the Driver Station
- [ ] Autonomous selected and confirmed with alliance partners
- [ ] Match strategy agreed with alliance partners
- [ ] Controllers plugged in, backup ready
- [ ] Safety glasses on

## After each match
1. Note any damage or errors for the pit.
2. Log battery and match number.
3. Debrief for two minutes: what worked, what to change.
