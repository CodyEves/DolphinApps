## At a glance
1. **Arrive:** sign in on the shop screen (see Signing in and out)
2. **Work:** your session is recorded (see Signing in and out)
3. **Leave:** sign out before you go (see Signing in and out)
4. **Can't come:** post in your absence channel (see Reporting an absence)
5. **Review:** leads check open sign-ins (see Daily review)

## Purpose and scope
Attendance tells mentors who is showing up, which matters for 5199 selection, travel, varsity letters and safety (we must know who is in the building). This SOP covers sign-in through the team app and absence reporting in Slack.

## Signing in and out
1. When you arrive, sign in on the shop sign-in screen before you start work. [Student]
2. Work in the room where you are signed in. Tell your lead if you move rooms for long.
3. KEY: Sign out before you leave, every time. Leaving signed in creates a record that a lead must fix by hand.
4. If the sign-in screen is down, post your arrival and departure times in [EDIT: channel] so a lead can enter them.

NOTICE: The system closes every open session automatically at [EDIT: time]. Anyone still signed in is flagged for review in #sign-in-records.

## Reporting an absence
1. Post in the right channel: #5199-class-absence or #9271-class-absences. [Student]
2. Say whether you will be absent, late or leaving early, and the reason in a few words.
3. Post before the session starts. Same-day notice is the minimum; planned conflicts need [EDIT: confirm notice period, e.g. 2 weeks].
4. Known competition conflicts go on your 5199 application, not only in Slack.

TIP: "Won't make it tonight, two tests tomorrow" is enough. You don't need to share private details.

## Daily review
1. Each morning, the Operations Lead reads the auto-close summary in #sign-in-records. [Lead]
2. For each student left signed in, confirm their real departure time and correct the record.
3. Students who repeatedly forget to sign out get a reminder; a pattern goes to a mentor. [Mentor]

## Records

| Auto-close summaries | #sign-in-records (private) | Mentors, leads |
| Absence posts | Absence channels | Team |
