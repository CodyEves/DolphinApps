Impact Award Submission

The Impact submission is built over months: story first, then each section against the judging rubric, then outside review, then the presentation. Deadlines are not suggestions.

01Impact gates

1
Gate 1

Story

  1. Agree on the team's story: who we are, what we changed and for whom.
  2. Build a schedule working back from FIRST's submission deadline: this year's deadline.
2
Gate 2

Sections

  1. Assign each information section an owner.
  2. Write to the current rubric. Judges use it first to shortlist teams, so every answer should clearly meet a criterion. Impact judges
  3. Focus on facts and evidence first; polish wording later.
3
Gate 3

Essay

  1. Write the rough draft with the story built in.
  2. Hold a work check before the draft deadline.
4
Gate 4

Review

  1. Ask experienced Impact judges and alumni to review the essay and presentation.
  2. Programs run by experienced teams also offer Impact mentoring.
5
Gate 5

Submit

  1. A mentor reads the final version before it's submitted. Mentor
  2. Submit before the deadline. Save a copy.
6
Gate 6

Present

  1. Practice the presentation and judges' questions.
  2. Prepare the awards cheat sheet for pit ambassadors: team facts, outreach numbers, robot highlights.
