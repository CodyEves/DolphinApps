## During the print
1. KEY: A printer may run while you are in the building, but someone checks it at least every [EDIT: minutes]. Long overnight prints need mentor approval. [Team rule]
2. Stop a print that is spaghetti-ing or lifting off the bed.

WARNING: Hot ends and beds can burn you. Never touch the nozzle, and let the bed cool before removing parts.

## Finishing
