summary: All parts are requested through the Slack purchase workflow with a link, part number and quantity. Purchases need approval before buying, and reimbursements go through Dana Hills Boosters.

## At a glance
1. **Check stock:** look before you order (see Before you request)
2. **Request:** Slack workflow (see Submitting a request)
3. **Approve:** mentor approves (see Approval and ordering)
4. **Order:** purchaser buys (see Approval and ordering)
5. **Receive:** check and store (see Receiving)
6. **Reimburse:** boosters process (see Reimbursement)

## Before you request
1. Check the shop and the inventory (RDOS-SOP-SHOP-003). Many fasteners and bearings are already on hand.
2. Group small orders from the same vendor where possible to save shipping.

## Submitting a request
Use the bookmarked workflow in #purchase-requests. Fill in every field: [Team rule]
- **Team:** choose 5199 or 9271 (or the B-team number) from the list, not free text
- **Item:** full description
- **Part number:** vendor part number (for example a McMaster or WCP number)
- **Quantity:** how many packs or pieces
- **Link:** direct product link
- **Notes:** what it's for, for example "hopper floor, Oil Rig"; mark comp-bot-only parts

NOTICE: Don't post conversations in #purchase-requests. Reply only in the thread under the request.

## Approval and ordering
1. A mentor approves or questions each request. [Mentor]
2. The purchaser orders approved items and replies in the thread with the expected delivery date.
3. Big purchases above [EDIT: approval limit] need [EDIT: approver] approval before ordering.

## Receiving
1. Check the delivery against the request.
2. Store it following RDOS-SOP-SHOP-003 and tell the requester in the thread.

## Reimbursement
1. Anyone who buys something for the team needs approval before buying. Unapproved purchases may not be reimbursed. [Team rule]
2. Submit the receipt through the Dana Hills Boosters reimbursement process by [EDIT: deadline]. [Source: danahillsboosters.com/reimbursement]
3. The reimbursement approver is listed in the Annual Insert.
