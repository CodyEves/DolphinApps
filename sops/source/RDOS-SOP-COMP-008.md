## Setup checklist

- [ ] Robot cart in place with room to work around it

- [ ] Tools out of the pit bags and in their spots
- [ ] Safety glasses available at the pit entrance
- [ ] Walkways and fire lanes clear
- [ ] Display screens powered and showing content
