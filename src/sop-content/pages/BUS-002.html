Weekly Business Meeting

The business team meets weekly with a shared notes canvas. Updates go in before the meeting so the time is spent deciding, not reporting.

01Before the meeting

  1. The Business Lead posts the time and room in #business on the meeting day.
  2. Everyone adds their updates to the meeting notes canvas before the meeting.

02Running the meeting

  1. Review open items: sponsorships, grants, fundraising, merch, Impact, outreach.
  2. Make decisions and assign every task an owner and a date.
  3. Start a huddle so remote members can join.

03After the meeting

  1. Owners update the canvas and tracker.
  2. Anything needing mentor or parent action goes into the next parent communication (RDOS-SOP-BUS-004).
