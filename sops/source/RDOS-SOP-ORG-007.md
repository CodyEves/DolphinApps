summary: How officers and mentors record a conduct incident in a factual, private way, and when it moves to mentors, families or school officials.

## At a glance
1. **Stop:** end the behavior safely (see During an incident)
2. **Record:** factual write-up (see Writing it up)
3. **Inform:** mentors the same day (see Escalation)
4. **Follow up:** coaching or consequences (see Escalation)

## During an incident
1. Calmly stop the behavior. If anyone is unsafe, remove them from the shop and get a mentor.
2. An officer may remove a student from the shop for the night with mentor agreement.

## Writing it up
Post in the private officers or mentors channel, the same day:
- **What happened:** specific behaviors, not opinions about the person
- **When and where**
- **Who saw it**
- **What was done:** warnings given, removal from shop
- **Who agrees:** officers and mentors who support the decision

NOTICE: Keep records private. Don't discuss incidents in public channels or with other students.

## Escalation
1. Follow handbook section 8.4: reminder, coaching conversation, written warning, parent contact, and so on. Serious issues may skip steps.
2. Suspected abuse, harassment that triggers mandated reporting, or an immediate threat goes through the school, CCA or district's official channel, not this process (handbook 7.5). [Mentor]
