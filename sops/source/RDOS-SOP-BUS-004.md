summary: Officers draft regular updates to parents, a mentor reviews them, and they go out by a fixed date. Parents should never be surprised by a deadline.

## At a glance
1. **Set date:** send date agreed (see Planning)
2. **Draft:** officers write it (see Drafting)
3. **Review:** mentor approves (see Review and send)
4. **Send:** by the deadline (see Review and send)

## Planning
1. Officers agree on the next send date, working back from important deadlines.
2. Collect topics from the business meeting, mentors and subteam leads.

## Drafting
Use the shared parent email document: [EDIT: location]. Every update covers, as needed:
- **Fundraising:** status and deadlines
- **Schedule:** last class, parent meetings, Kickoff and events
- **Events:** timing, agenda and travel
- **Applications:** status of the 5199 process
- **Recaps:** recent events, outreach and photos
- **Action items:** what parents must do, with dates

## Review and send
1. A mentor reviews the draft at least [EDIT: days] before the send date. [Mentor]
2. Send from the team address to the parent list. Post a short version in the parent GroupMe.
3. Save a copy in the parent email document.

NOTICE: Don't include grades, discipline, medical information or other private details. Private matters go to that family only.
