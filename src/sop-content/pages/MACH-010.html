Manufacturing Request Workflow

Every part that needs making goes through a manufacturing request in Slack with a file, quantity and due date. One person claims it, makes it, and closes it out.

02Requesting a part

  1. Make sure the CAD is final and the part studio is named clearly (see RDOS-SOP-DES-001).
  2. Submit the Manufacturing Request form in #manufacturing with:
Part name

as it appears in CAD

Team

5199, 9271 or the B-team number

Quantity

including spares

Due date

when it's needed, not "ASAP"

Material and thickness

for example 1/8 in. 6061 aluminum, 1/16 in. polycarbonate

Link

Onshape link or exported file

  1. Answer questions in the request's thread, not in a new message.

03Claiming and making

  1. The maker clicks I'll Make This! in the request. One maker per request.
  2. CAM and run the part following the machine's SOP and RDOS-SOP-MACH-011.
  3. Inspect the part against the drawing: critical holes, thickness, deburring.
  4. If you can't finish by the due date, say so in the thread right away.

04Delivering

  1. Bag or label the part with its name and request date.
  2. Post a photo in the thread and hand the part to the requester or put it in delivery bin.
  3. The requester confirms the part fits, then the request is closed.

05Queue rules

  • Competition robot parts come before practice and B-team parts unless a mentor says otherwise.
  • Requests with no claim after hours get assigned by the Manufacturing Lead.
