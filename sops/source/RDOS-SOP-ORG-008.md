## At a glance
1. **Own:** team addresses own everything (see Ownership)
2. **Store:** one protected credentials list (see Credentials)
3. **Grant:** access through groups (see Access)
4. **Review:** each season (see Annual review)

## Credentials
1. KEY: Passwords, radio passwords and logins live only in [EDIT: protected credentials list]. Never post them in Slack, canvases, SOPs or documents shared with students. [Team rule]
2. If a password is posted anywhere it shouldn't be, change it that day.
3. Turn on two-factor authentication for every account that offers it.

## Access
1. Grant access by adding people to a group (for example the media group gives access to the media drive), not by sharing individual files.
2. Use school-independent emails only when a person needs access after leaving school.

## Annual review
1. At the end of each season, remove graduated students and departed mentors from groups and accounts.
2. Confirm who owns each account and update the table above.
