01Requirements

this year's lettering requirements from the Annual Insert

  • Competition days: e.g. 6 days total. Offseason events count. A missing competition day can be made up with practice day hours.
  • Shop hours during competition weeks: e.g. 12 hours between Monday and the event. Competition hours do not count as shop hours.
  • Outreach events: number.

02Weekly reporting

  1. Every week the mentor posts a reminder in #lettering.
  2. Reply in the thread by the end of class (8 pm) with your dates, tasks and leadership notes. Reports outside the thread are not counted. Team rule
  3. If you can't make your hours in a week, tell the mentor before the practice day.

03Deadlines

  1. Check your status with the mentor at least two weeks before the deadline, in person, not over Slack.
  2. Requirements must be complete by date. The mentor reports to schools by date.
  3. Letters are distributed by each school's athletic department. If yours doesn't arrive, tell the mentor.
