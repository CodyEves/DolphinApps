summary: Practice on full fields at other teams' facilities. Book the slot, collect forms, transport the robot and battery cart, test the programmers' list, and record everything.

## At a glance
1. **Book:** request the slot (see Booking)
2. **Forms:** collect by the deadline (see Before you go)
3. **Pack:** robot, batteries, pit bag (see Before you go)
4. **Practice:** test list (see At the field)
5. **Report:** video and notes (see After practice)

## Booking
1. Request a slot with the host team's practice field coordinator (for example through their sign-up form).
2. Confirm date, time and how many people may attend.

## Before you go
1. Collect permission forms from everyone attending by [EDIT: deadline]. No form, no trip. [Team rule]
2. Post the attendee list and transportation plan for the robot and battery cart.
3. Charge every battery and pack the pit bag with tools.
4. Programmers post the test list (for example climb, red and blue autos, shooting on the move).

## At the field
1. Follow the host team's rules.
2. Work through the test list in order. Swap drivers to build depth.
3. Record video of practice runs. Photos of other robots are fine where the host allows.

## After practice
1. Post videos and test results in #drive-team-practice.
2. Turn failures into tasks.
3. Thank the host team.
