## At a glance
1. **Stop work:** 20 minutes before close (see Closing procedure)
2. **Tools home:** every tool back in its spot (see Closing procedure)
3. **Parts binned:** labeled bins onto shelves (see Closing procedure)
4. **Surfaces clear:** tables, floor, machines (see Closing procedure)
5. **Room check:** a Lead walks each room (see Room check)
6. **Lock up:** mentor closes the rooms (see Closing procedure)

## Purpose and scope
A clean shop is a safe shop. This SOP covers closing every room the team uses at the end of each session: B9, C4, C6, C7 and E1. It applies during build season too, when every shop day matters. [Team rule]

## Rules
- All tools go back where they belong, no exceptions.
- All parts go in labeled bins, then into the cabinets or onto the B9 shelf.
- No parts or bins are left on the B9 tables, the driver station, or the C4 and C6 tables.
- Cleaning is everyone's job. You may not leave until you have cleaned something.
- Never block a fire extinguisher, exit or breaker panel, even for a minute.

WARNING: If any room is not fully clean when we leave, shop access is removed for the entire team the following day. The rooms will not be opened. [Team rule]

## Roles
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| [Student] Everyone | Cleans their own work area, then helps with a shared area |
| [Lead] Subteam lead | Confirms what their subteam is still working on and that it's binned and labeled |
| [Lead] Safety Captain | Runs the room check and reports problems |
| [Mentor] Closing mentor | Does the final walk and locks the rooms |

## Closing procedure
1. At [EDIT: time, e.g. 20 minutes before close], the closing Lead announces cleanup in every room.
2. Stop all machines and run each machine's own shutdown steps (see the machine SOPs).
3. Return every tool to its labeled spot. Missing tool? Tell the Safety Captain before you leave.
4. Put parts in a labeled bin: subteam, mechanism, owner, next step. Bins go in cabinets or on the B9 shelf.
5. Clear and wipe every table. Sweep chips and offcuts. Empty overfull trash.
6. Put batteries on the charging cart, not on tables. Close laptops and return them to the cart.
7. Check with your subteam lead before leaving. If your area is done, clean a different one.
8. KEY: The closing mentor walks every room, confirms the exits, extinguishers and breaker panels are clear, and locks up.

## When the check fails
1. The Safety Captain names the room and what was left out in Slack.
2. The closing mentor decides whether the next day's shop is closed. [Mentor]
3. Repeat problems are handled through the safety infraction process (RDOS-SOP-SAFE-001).
