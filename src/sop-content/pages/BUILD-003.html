01Before you request

  1. Check the shop and the inventory (RDOS-SOP-SHOP-003). Many fasteners and bearings are already on hand.
  2. Group small orders from the same vendor where possible to save shipping.

02Submitting a request

Use the bookmarked workflow in #purchase-requests. Fill in every field: Team rule

Team

choose 5199 or 9271 (or the B-team number) from the list, not free text

Item

full description

Part number

vendor part number (for example a McMaster or WCP number)

Quantity

how many packs or pieces

Link

direct product link

Notes

what it's for, for example "hopper floor, Oil Rig"; mark comp-bot-only parts

NOTICE

Don't post conversations in #purchase-requests. Reply only in the thread under the request.

03Approval and ordering

  1. A mentor approves or questions each request. Mentor
  2. The purchaser orders approved items and replies in the thread with the expected delivery date.
  3. Big purchases above approval limit need approver approval before ordering.

04Receiving

  1. Check the delivery against the request.
  2. Store it following RDOS-SOP-SHOP-003 and tell the requester in the thread.

05Reimbursement

  1. Anyone who buys something for the team needs approval before buying. Unapproved purchases may not be reimbursed. Team rule
  2. Submit the receipt through the Dana Hills Boosters reimbursement process by deadline. danahillsboosters.com/reimbursement
  3. The reimbursement approver is listed in the Annual Insert.
