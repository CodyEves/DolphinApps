Annual Parent Fundraising and Corporate Matching

Once a year, the program runs a parent fundraising campaign with an online donation page and a live progress graphic, and pushes corporate matching to double gifts.

01Campaign gates

1
Gate 1

Set up

  1. Create or update the campaign page on the fundraising platform (currently Givebutter). Set the goal, description and progress graphic.
  2. Connect it to the booster organization (Dana Hills Boosters, a 501(c)(3)) so donations are processed properly.
  3. Set up the sending domain so campaign emails come from the team's address. Mentor
  4. Link the campaign on the team website.
2
Gate 2

Launch

  1. Send the kickoff email to all parents with the link.
  2. Post in #announcements asking students to make sure their parents read it.
3
Gate 3

Matching push

  1. Remind families that many employers match donations or volunteer hours (for example through Benevity).
  2. Give clear instructions, deadlines and examples. Gifts often must be made by December 31.
4
Gate 4

Close and thank

  1. Send thank-you notes and tax receipts through the boosters.
  2. Report the total in the next parent communication.
