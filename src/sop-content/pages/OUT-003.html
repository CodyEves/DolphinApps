Running a Student-Led Class or Camp

Student instructors plan a short course for younger students (such as Tech Trek) around a calendar and a capstone project the students show their parents.

01Course gates

1
Gate 1

Plan

  1. Agree the calendar with the partner organization. Every session needs a supervising adult. Mentor
  2. Plan a capstone project, for example designing and building a small catapult.
2
Gate 2

Prepare

  1. Each session has a lead instructor and helpers.
  2. Prepare materials, laptops and machine time in advance.
3
Gate 3

Teach

  1. Open with something engaging: a robot demo, the team's robot history, or how team roles work in a match.
  2. Let the students do the work. Instructors guide.
  3. Watch 3D prints and CNC jobs between sessions so parts are ready.
4
Gate 4

Capstone

  1. Project-manage so every group has something to test.
  2. Invite parents to the last session to see the projects tested.
  3. Send the partner organization photos and a short recap.
