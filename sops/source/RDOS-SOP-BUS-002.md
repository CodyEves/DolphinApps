title: Weekly Business Meeting
dept: Business
style: Procedure
owner: Business Lead
audience: Public
summary: The business team meets weekly with a shared notes canvas. Updates go in before the meeting so the time is spent deciding, not reporting.

## At a glance
1. **Announce:** time and room (see Before the meeting)
2. **Pre-fill:** updates in the canvas (see Before the meeting)
3. **Meet:** decisions and assignments (see Running the meeting)
4. **Follow up:** owners and dates (see After the meeting)

## Before the meeting
1. The Business Lead posts the time and room in #business on the meeting day.
2. Everyone adds their updates to the meeting notes canvas before the meeting.

## Running the meeting
1. Review open items: sponsorships, grants, fundraising, merch, Impact, outreach.
2. Make decisions and assign every task an owner and a date.
3. Start a huddle so remote members can join.

## After the meeting
1. Owners update the canvas and tracker.
2. Anything needing mentor or parent action goes into the next parent communication (RDOS-SOP-BUS-004).
