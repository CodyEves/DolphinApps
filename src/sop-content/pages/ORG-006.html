01Debrief gates

1
Gate 1

Debrief

  1. Each subteam meets after the last event: what worked, what failed, what to change.
  2. Record notes in debrief folder.
2
Gate 2

Robot record

Write the annual robot record (handbook section 15.1):

  • Game name and season
  • Robot name and final photo
  • Strategy and robot functions
  • Major mechanisms and technical highlights
  • Programming, autonomous, scouting and controls highlights
  • Event results and awards
  • What we would repeat and what we would change
  • Credits to students, mentors and sponsors
3
Gate 3

Handoff

  1. Outgoing leaders and graduating seniors leave training notes, CAD and code links, sponsor status and outreach contacts.
  2. Meet with the incoming leader in person.
4
Gate 4

Update SOPs

  1. Each subteam reviews its SOPs and suggests changes in the base copies.
  2. Mentors approve, and the final pages are rebuilt (RDOS-SOP-ORG-000).

Copy this SOP
