4. **Gate 4:** finish (see Gate 4: Finish)

## Tracks
| Track | Format | Finish line |
|---|---|---|
| Design / CAD | About 10 weekly online assignments plus review sessions | Design challenge reviewed by the CAD Lead |
| Manufacturing / CAM and CNC | About 5 weeks of online CAM lessons, then about 5 weeks of in-person CNC training | Haas certification (RDOS-SOP-MACH-002) |
| Electrical / Control systems | In-person lessons and builds | Wired subsystem signed off |
| Programming | Self-paced lesson plan | Working subsystem code |
| Business | Projects with the business mentor | Project delivered |

## Training gates
### Gate 1: Pick a track
1. Everyone picks one track when trainings are announced, usually by reacting to the post.

### Gate 2: Prerequisites
1. Complete the track's prerequisites before the first session. For example, CAD starts with FRCDesign.org Stage 0 and Stage 1A; CNC needs the Haas Basic Mill Operator course.

### Gate 3: Weekly lessons
1. New assignments drop on [EDIT: day] with a recommended due date. Classes are self-paced, but keep up.
2. Store work where the instructor asks (for CAD, your named folder in Onshape).
3. Review sessions answer questions and introduce the next lesson.

### Gate 4: Finish
1. Complete the finishing project or certification.
2. Sign-offs are recorded in the training tool.
