## At a glance
1. **Gate 0:** forms and shop safety (see Gate 0: Shop safety)
2. **Gate 1:** hand and power tools (see Gate 1: Hand and power tools)
3. **Gate 2:** machine operator (see Gate 2: Machine operator)
4. **Gate 3:** machine lead (see Gate 3: Machine lead)

## Purpose and scope
The handbook says students may not use tools, machines, batteries, robots or field equipment until they are trained and approved. This SOP sets the gates. Each machine SOP lists its own skills for Gates 2 and 3.

## Certification gates
### Gate 0: Shop safety
- [ ] Handbook acknowledgment and Team Safety Waiver signed
- [ ] General shop safety lesson completed
- [ ] Shop safety test passed in the training tool
- [ ] Knows where the exits, extinguishers, first aid kit and eye wash are

Deadline: before using any tool, and no later than September 30. [Team rule]

### Gate 1: Hand and power tools
- [ ] Measuring tools and layout (calipers, rules, decimal conversion)
- [ ] Hand tools: names, correct use, safety
- [ ] Fasteners and rivets
- [ ] Cordless power tools and lithium batteries
- [ ] Signed off by a Lead or mentor in the training tool

### Gate 2: Machine operator
- [ ] Read the machine's SOP
- [ ] Completed any outside course the SOP requires (for the Haas mill, the Haas Basic Mill Operator course with all quizzes passed)
- [ ] Passed the machine's safety test
- [ ] Completed supervised runs listed in the SOP
- [ ] Signed off by a Lead or mentor

### Gate 3: Machine lead
- [ ] At least [EDIT: number] sessions as an operator
- [ ] Completed the lead tasks in the machine SOP under supervision
- [ ] Appointed by a mentor [Mentor]

## Keeping certification
1. A safety infraction on a machine can suspend that machine's sign-off until retraining. [Team rule]
2. An operator who has not used a machine for [EDIT: months] repeats the supervised run.
3. Sign-offs and tests live in the training tool. Paper cards are not used.
