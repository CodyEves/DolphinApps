Scouting, Strategy Meetings and Pick Lists

Scout every match, meet each night to turn data into a pick list, and keep the stands feeding notes right up to alliance selection.

01Before the event

  1. Choose the scouting system: our own app, a Google form, or a scouting alliance with another team.
  2. If joining a scouting alliance, every scout reads the partner's guide and fills in their access form before the event.
  3. Post the scouting shift schedule. Scouts must know how to use the app before the first match.
  4. Teammates not at the event can scout from the livestream.

02During qualifications

  1. Scouts cover their shifts; swap with someone if you must leave, and tell the Strategy Lead.
  2. Pit scouts and Pod Squad (RDOS-SOP-COMP-006) post notes on teams they check on.
  3. Write qualitative notes: defense, feeding, driver skill, reliability, penalties.

03Nightly strategy meeting

  1. Start a huddle in #scouting-strategy so remote members can join.
  2. Review data and video for each candidate team.
  3. Update the pick list, including a list of likely third and fourth picks.

04Pick list

ListWhat goes on it
First picksTeams that complement our robot best
Second / third picksRanked list, usually 8 or more teams, with a note on each
DNP (do not pick)Teams with repeated no-shows, safety issues or serious reliability problems, with the reason
NOTICE

Keep DNP reasons factual and private to the team. Never share them publicly.

05Alliance selection

  1. On the last morning, post the current list and ask everyone in the stands to send notes on listed teams.
  2. The Strategy Lead and drive coach make the final list. Lead
  3. Thank every scout.
