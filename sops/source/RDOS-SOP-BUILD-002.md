## At a glance
1. **Work:** shop session (see Purpose)
2. **Write:** update in the format (see Format)
3. **Post:** team's nightly channel (see Where and when)
4. **Read:** react when read (see Where and when)

## Purpose
Nightly updates tell members who couldn't come what happened in a subsystem. It is the subsystem lead's job to make sure everyone knows what is happening in their subsystem. [Team rule]

## Format
Copy this and fill it in:
- **Date:** day and date
- **Subsystem:** for example Hopper Floor, Programming, Outreach
- **Members:** everyone who worked on it (tag them)
- **Progress:** what got done, specific enough to act on
- **Problems:** what went wrong and what was tried
- **Next steps:** what the next person should do first
- **Photos or video:** whenever possible

NOTICE: "I don't want to go into too much detail" is not an update. Write enough that someone could pick up the work tomorrow without asking you.

## Where and when
1. Post in #5199-nightly-updates or #9271-nightly-updates.
2. KEY: Post before the next shop session. The same night is best.
3. If the lead is absent, they make sure someone who was there posts it.
4. Leads read every update for their subsystem.
