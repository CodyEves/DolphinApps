summary: New mentors complete every required clearance before working with students, then get a guided start. Every session and event has adult supervision.

## At a glance
1. **Meet:** visit and conversation (see Recruiting)
2. **Clear:** required screening and training (see Clearances)
3. **Welcome:** introduced to the team (see First weeks)
4. **Cover:** sign up for supervision (see Supervision coverage)

## Recruiting
1. Prospective mentors visit a class and meet the instructors.
2. Programs such as College Corps place college students as mentors for a set number of hours each week.
3. If a mentor or student is coming from another team, talk with that team's lead mentor first so it doesn't look like poaching.

- [ ] FIRST Youth Protection screening complete
- [ ] [EDIT: CCA and district requirements, e.g. fingerprinting, mandated reporter training]
- [ ] Handbook read
- [ ] Added to Slack and #mentors

## First weeks
1. Introduce the new mentor in #mentors and to students in class.
2. Pair them with an experienced mentor in their area.
3. A mentor's schedule may vary; they post absences in #mentors.

## Supervision coverage
1. Every class, shop session, outreach event and student-led session needs an adult present. [Team rule]
2. When coverage is missing, post the date, time and channel in #mentors as early as possible.
3. Remote mentors support design and planning but don't count as on-site supervision.
