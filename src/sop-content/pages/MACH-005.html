01Safety essentials

Never

  • Hold a part by hand while milling or drilling
  • Wear gloves near the spindle
  • Leave a wrench or drawbar key on the machine
  • Reach past a turning cutter
WARNING

Spinning cutters and flying chips. Keep the chip shield in place and wait for the spindle to stop before clearing chips.

03Operation

  1. Clamp the part in the vise on parallels; tap it down.
  2. Calculate spindle speed and feed for the cutter and material. A speeds-and-feeds calculator (for example fswizard.com) is fine; write the numbers down. fswizard
  3. Lock the axes you are not moving.
  4. Start the spindle and take light cuts.
  5. Stop the spindle before measuring or clearing chips.

04Shutdown and cleanup

  1. Remove the cutter and drawbar wrench.
  2. Brush chips off the table and vise. Oil the ways.

05Training and sign-off

  1. Read this SOP; pass the mill safety test
  2. Supervised drilling and slotting to a dimension
  3. Shows correct speeds-and-feeds calculation
