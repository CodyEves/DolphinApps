Parent Communication

Officers draft regular updates to parents, a mentor reviews them, and they go out by a fixed date. Parents should never be surprised by a deadline.

01Planning

  1. Officers agree on the next send date, working back from important deadlines.
  2. Collect topics from the business meeting, mentors and subteam leads.

02Drafting

Use the shared parent email document: location. Every update covers, as needed:

Fundraising

status and deadlines

Schedule

last class, parent meetings, Kickoff and events

Events

timing, agenda and travel

Applications

status of the 5199 process

Recaps

recent events, outreach and photos

Action items

what parents must do, with dates

03Review and send

  1. A mentor reviews the draft at least days before the send date. Mentor
  2. Send from the team address to the parent list. Post a short version in the parent GroupMe.
  3. Save a copy in the parent email document.
NOTICE

Don't include grades, discipline, medical information or other private details. Private matters go to that family only.
