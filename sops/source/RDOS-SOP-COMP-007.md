## Shifts
1. Check the shift schedule the night before: pit, scouting, volunteering, spirit cart, Pod Squad.
2. Swap with someone if you must leave, and tell the shift's lead.
3. No job right now? Go to the stands and scout or cheer.
4. Fulfil your role before socializing, shirt trading or visiting other pits (handbook 8.3).

## During matches
1. Keep the stands, pit and eating areas clean.
2. Answer roll calls and RSVPs in the event channel right away.
3. Team photos are called in the channel; come immediately.

## Closing the event
1. KEY: The team always stays to help put away the field. [Team rule]
2. Pack the pit following RDOS-SOP-COMP-001.
3. Team photo after cleanup. If you are not in the photo, you do not get credit for the event (except members who could only attend another day). [Team rule]

## Team meals
1. RSVP to the meal post so the organizer can book the right size table.
2. If someone pays for a group, they post the split in #tax-collector with each person's amount. Pay them back promptly.
