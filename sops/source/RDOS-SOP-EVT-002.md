summary: Students volunteer at FLL, FTC and FRC events as field reset, scoring and queuing helpers. No experience is needed; show up on time and follow the event's volunteer lead.

## At a glance
1. **Sign up:** respond to the request (see Signing up)
2. **Prepare:** time, place, dress (see Before the event)
3. **Serve:** follow the volunteer lead (see At the event)
4. **Log:** record hours (see After the event)

## Signing up
1. Volunteer requests are posted in #announcements. React or reply to sign up.
2. Some events use their own volunteer system. Sign up there and tell the Connections Director.

## Before the event
1. Check the address carefully. Some events are at other schools.
2. Wear team clothing and closed-toe shoes.

## At the event
1. Check in with the event's volunteer coordinator.
2. Common jobs: field reset, scoring, queuing.
3. Represent the team with Gracious Professionalism. Younger students are watching you.

## After the event
1. Log volunteer hours in [EDIT: outreach hours log].
