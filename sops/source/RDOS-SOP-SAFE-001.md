## At a glance
1. **Spot:** an unsafe act is seen (see Recording an infraction)
2. **Record:** posted in #safety-cam (see Recording an infraction)
3. **Serve:** work it off within 2 weeks (see Serving an infraction)
4. **Escalate:** unserved infractions add up (see Escalation)
5. **Review:** patterns go to mentors (see Escalation)

## Purpose and scope
This SOP puts handbook section 9.2 into practice. It covers what counts as an infraction, how it is recorded and served, and how it affects eligibility.

## What counts as an infraction
[EDIT: final list of infraction definitions, approved by mentors]

- No eye protection in a work area or pit
- Open-toe shoes in a work area
- Using a tool or machine without sign-off
- Unsafe tool use, horseplay or throwing objects in the shop
- Food or drink at a machine, battery or electronics station
- Blocking an exit, extinguisher or breaker panel
- Leaving a running machine unattended

## Recording an infraction
1. A mentor, Safety Captain or Lead sees the unsafe act and stops it first. Safety before paperwork.
2. Post the infraction in #safety-cam: who, what rule, when. A photo helps but is not required.
3. The Safety Captain logs it in [EDIT: infraction log location]. [Lead]

NOTICE: Infractions are about the behavior, not the person. Keep posts factual and never post a photo of an injury.

## Serving an infraction
You have **two weeks** to clear an infraction by one of these: [Team rule]

1. Complete a mentor-assigned task that is not robot work (organizing, cleaning, inventory). The task is done before you return to robot work.
2. Catch someone else breaking a safety rule and pass one infraction on to them. You may pass only one at a time. This option runs on trust; if it is abused, mentors will remove it.

## Escalation
1. An infraction not served within two weeks adds a second infraction, and another every two weeks after that.
2. No member may have more than three infractions in a school year. A fourth means a mentor meeting and possible loss of tool access. [Mentor]
3. Even after it is served, an infraction stays on the record for lettering, leadership, tool access, pit crew, drive team, 5199 selection and travel.
