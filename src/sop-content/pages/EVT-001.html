01Purpose

Beach Blitz is an all-hands event and a big part of the program's public identity. Every student enrolled in a CCA robotics class is expected to volunteer. Team rule

02Hosting gates

1
Gate 1

Commit and plan

  1. Confirm dates with the co-host, the school and the venue. Mentor
  2. Set up the event channel and planning canvas.
  3. Book the field, field electronics and practice field.
  4. Plan the facility: pits, stands, admin office, food, parking.
2
Gate 2

Staff it

  1. Build the volunteer schedule in 2-hour shifts on a sign-up site.
  2. Announce the sign-up with a deadline. Remind everyone a week before.
  3. After the deadline, assign anyone who didn't sign up to open shifts.
  4. Students who registered directly with the event as volunteers don't need extra shifts.
3
Gate 3

Load-in

  1. Set up the field to spec, pits and the practice field.
  2. Confirm safety glasses, first aid and emergency plans with the venue.
4
Gate 4

Run the event

  1. Volunteers check in for their shifts on time.
  2. Leads post updates and calls for help in the event channel.
  3. Students on duty stay at their stations; anyone free supports the team in the stands.
5
Gate 5

Restore the venue

  1. Everyone stays to put away the field. Team rule
  2. Clear the pits, admin office, halls and every other area we used.
  3. Field electronics and AprilTags are never taken home. Other field items may be, if the director allows.
  4. Return the school to its previous state before leaving.
6
Gate 6

Debrief

  1. Send the feedback survey to teams and volunteers.
  2. Hold a debrief within weeks and record what to repeat and change.
  3. Update this SOP.

Copy this SOP

Team 5199 and Team 9271 publish this manual so other teams can copy and adapt it. Change the names, numbers and blanks to fit your shop.
