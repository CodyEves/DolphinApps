summary: Team 5199 is selected each year through an application and interview. This SOP shows how the process runs, from opening the application to announcing the roster before winter break.

## At a glance
1. **Gate 1:** open applications (see Gate 1: Open)
2. **Gate 2:** collect evidence (see Gate 2: Evidence)
3. **Gate 3:** interviews (see Gate 3: Interviews)
4. **Gate 4:** selection (see Gate 4: Selection)
5. **Gate 5:** announce (see Gate 5: Announce)

## Purpose
This puts handbook section 4.3 into practice. Placement is a program decision based on reliability, capacity, availability, character, safety and team needs. It is not a measure of personal worth.

## Selection gates
### Gate 1: Open
1. Publish the timeline, criteria and any capacity limit by October.
2. Open the application form in September. Applicants list known competition conflicts on it.
3. Send a reminder 10 days before the deadline.

### Gate 2: Evidence
1. Mentors review every application.
2. Pull participation evidence: shop sign-ins outside class, outreach, Beach Blitz shifts, nightly updates and safety record. Sign-in data can have gaps, so compare against students who were signing in at the time.
3. Ask other mentors for feedback on students they have worked with.

### Gate 3: Interviews
1. Applicants book through the interview form by the stated date.
2. Schedule 15-minute slots: about 10 minutes of interview and a 5-minute buffer.
3. Publish the schedule and confirm with students before each interview day.
4. At least two adults sit on every interview. [Mentor]

### Gate 4: Selection
1. Score against the handbook categories: reliability, capacity, availability and character.
2. If a cap is used, qualified Level 3 and 4 students get priority.

### Gate 5: Announce
1. Announce the roster before winter break.
2. Give students who were not selected a respectful explanation and next steps. CCA students continue with 9271.
