01Purpose

Gracious Professionalism in action. Helping other teams keeps more robots on the field and builds friendships across the community. It also gives our scouts useful information about other robots.

02The chest

  1. The chest has an inventory list printed inside the lid: inventory.
  2. Every item has a home in the chest layout so anyone can find it.

03Setup

  1. Set up the chest at load-in in location, e.g. our pit or a help desk.
  2. Lay out items according to the setup guide.

04Shifts

  1. Post a shift schedule. There is always a lead on duty, and a named substitute when the lead steps away.
  2. Members can also be sent to check on teams that look like they are struggling.

05Handling a request

  1. Ask what the team needs and what's wrong.
  2. Hand out parts from the chest, or bring a Pod Squad member to help.
  3. If we need to make a part, check with the Manufacturing Lead.
  4. Never give away competition robot spares without the Pit Lead's okay.

06The log

  1. Record every team helped in the Pod Squad log sheet: team number, problem, what we gave or did, time.
  2. Share useful observations (for example "their intake broke" or "browning out") with the Strategy Lead.

Copy this SOP

Team 5199 and Team 9271 publish this manual so other teams can copy and adapt it. Change the names, numbers and blanks to fit your shop.
