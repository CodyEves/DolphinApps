02Starting a print

  1. Printed robot parts start as a manufacturing request (RDOS-SOP-MACH-010).
  2. Slice with the team profile for the material: slicer and profile names.
  3. Choose strength settings for structural parts: walls, infill, orientation rules.
  4. Clean the build plate and confirm the right filament is loaded.
  5. Start the print and watch the first layer go down.

03During the print

  1. A printer may run while you are in the building, but someone checks it at least every minutes. Long overnight prints need mentor approval. Team rule
  2. Stop a print that is spaghetti-ing or lifting off the bed.
WARNING

Hot ends and beds can burn you. Never touch the nozzle, and let the bed cool before removing parts.

04Finishing

  1. Let the bed cool; remove the part and clean the plate.
  2. Post the finished part in the manufacturing request thread.
  3. Log failed prints and why in print log.
