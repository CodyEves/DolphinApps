## At a glance
1. **Drawings:** official field drawings (see Build)
2. **Build:** to dimension (see Build)
3. **Verify:** measure every critical dimension (see Verification)
4. **Approve:** field lead signs off (see Verification)
5. **Maintain:** after damage (see Maintenance)

## Build
1. Download the official field drawings and the team versions for budget builds.
2. Build critical elements first: the ones the robot must score in, drive under or climb.
3. Cheaper materials are fine if the critical dimensions and visibility match. For example, slots or translucent panels for driver visibility through a scoring element.

## Verification
1. Measure every dimension the robot interacts with (heights, openings, angles) against the drawings.
2. Record measurements in [EDIT: field log].
3. KEY: Fix anything out of tolerance before programmers test. A trench built 1/2 in. low can damage the robot.
4. The Field Lead confirms the field is set up to spec and bolted down before testing. [Lead]

## Maintenance
1. Check fasteners after heavy testing; bent screws and shifted elements happen.
2. Post damage in #field-assembly with a photo.
