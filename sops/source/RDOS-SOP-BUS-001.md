## Pipeline gates
### Gate 1: Find
1. Look for: vendor sponsorships (for example parts and apparel vendors), local businesses, city grants, foundations, and companies where families work.
2. Add each one to the tracker before starting work, so nobody duplicates it.

### Gate 2: Apply
1. Ask for a volunteer in #business and set a draft date before the deadline.
2. Use the team's standard materials: [EDIT: team description, numbers, photos folder].
3. KEY: Students never promise anything to a sponsor without adult approval. [Team rule]
4. A mentor reviews before it's sent. [Mentor]

### Gate 3: Follow up
1. Email first: thank them, introduce yourself, and say when you will call and why.
2. Call when you said you would. If there's no answer, leave a message and email again.
3. Log every contact in the tracker.

### Gate 4: Thank and report
1. Thank every sponsor within [EDIT: days] of an award.
2. Ask the media team for a thank-you post (RDOS-SOP-MEDIA-001).
3. Add the sponsor's logo to the website, pit display and robot as agreed.
