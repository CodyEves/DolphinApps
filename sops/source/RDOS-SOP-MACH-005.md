## At a glance
1. **Gear up:** glasses, no gloves (see Safety essentials)
2. **Clamp:** vise or clamps, never by hand (see Operation)
3. **Speeds & feeds:** calculate first (see Operation)
4. **Cut:** locks on unused axes (see Operation)
5. **Clean:** brush chips, oil ways (see Shutdown and cleanup)

## Safety essentials
CARD NO: Never
- Hold a part by hand while milling or drilling
- Wear gloves near the spindle
- Leave a wrench or drawbar key on the machine
- Reach past a turning cutter

WARNING: **Spinning cutters and flying chips.** Keep the chip shield in place and wait for the spindle to stop before clearing chips.

## Operation
1. Clamp the part in the vise on parallels; tap it down.
2. Calculate spindle speed and feed for the cutter and material. A speeds-and-feeds calculator (for example fswizard.com) is fine; write the numbers down. [Source: fswizard]
3. Lock the axes you are not moving.
4. GO: Start the spindle and take light cuts.
5. Stop the spindle before measuring or clearing chips.

## Shutdown and cleanup
1. Remove the cutter and drawbar wrench.
2. Brush chips off the table and vise. Oil the ways.

## Training and sign-off
1. Read this SOP; pass the mill safety test
2. Supervised drilling and slotting to a dimension
3. Shows correct speeds-and-feeds calculation
