summary: Lathe work is done by signed-off operators with a Lead present. Remove the chuck key every time, keep sleeves and hair clear, and stop the spindle before measuring.

## At a glance
1. **Gear up:** glasses, no gloves (see Safety essentials)
2. **Check:** chuck, key, guards (see Pre-use check)
3. **Mount:** stock tight, minimal stick-out (see Operation)
4. **Remove key:** every time (see Operation)
5. **Cut:** light passes, spindle speed for the material (see Operation)
6. **Clean:** brush, never by hand (see Shutdown and cleanup)

## Safety essentials
CARD NO: Never
- Leave the chuck key in the chuck
- Wear gloves, rings or loose sleeves
- Measure or file a spinning part by hand without mentor training
- Clear stringy chips with your hand

DANGER: **Entanglement.** A lathe can pull in a sleeve, glove or hair in less than a second. Tie hair back and stand out of line with the chuck when starting.

## Pre-use check
- [ ] PPE on; no gloves, jewelry or loose clothing
- [ ] Chuck key in its holder, not in the chuck
- [ ] Chuck guard and splash guard in place
- [ ] Tool bit sharp and tight; tool post locked
- [ ] Ways clear of tools and chips

## Operation
1. Mount the stock with the least stick-out that leaves room for the cut; tighten all jaws.
2. KEY: Remove the chuck key and put it in its holder.
3. Set spindle speed for the material and diameter using [EDIT: speed chart location].
4. GO: Start the spindle and take light passes. A common team job is rounding the ends of axles to fit bearings.
5. Stop the spindle completely before measuring.

## Shutdown and cleanup
1. Spindle off; remove the part and tools.
2. Brush chips off the ways and into the tray. Use a chip hook for stringy chips.
3. Oil the ways per [EDIT: maintenance schedule].

## Training and sign-off
1. Read this SOP; pass the lathe safety test
2. Pre-use check unprompted
3. Supervised facing and turning to a dimension
4. E-stop drill
