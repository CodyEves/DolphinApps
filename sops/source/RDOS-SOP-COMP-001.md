summary: Before every event the team asks one question: are we ready to compete and represent the Robot Dolphin Family well? Three gates, a week out, two days out and load-in day, make sure the answer is yes.

## At a glance
1. **Gate 1:** one week out (see Gate 1: One week out)
2. **Gate 2:** two days out (see Gate 2: Two days out)
3. **Gate 3:** load-in day (see Gate 3: Load-in day)
4. **Unpack:** after the event (see After the event)

## Readiness gates
### Gate 1: One week out
- [ ] Robot runs every match function reliably
- [ ] Software locked (RDOS-SOP-PROG-001)
- [ ] Spares list made and spare parts ordered or manufactured
- [ ] Drive team, pit crew, scouts and awards roles assigned
- [ ] Travel forms, hotel and food counts confirmed
- [ ] Awards materials ready (RDOS-SOP-BUS-006)

### Gate 2: Two days out
- [ ] All batteries charged and tested (RDOS-SOP-ELEC-001)
- [ ] Soak test done
- [ ] Bumpers in both colors packed
- [ ] Pit bags and competition box checked against their lists
- [ ] Scouting system and shift schedule posted
- [ ] Spirit, uniforms and safety glasses packed

### Gate 3: Load-in day
- [ ] Robot on cart, strapped
- [ ] Battery cart and chargers
- [ ] Driver Station laptop, controllers and backups
- [ ] Teardown kit (RDOS-SOP-COMP-002)
- [ ] Pit display and power strips
- [ ] First aid kit

## Packing lists
| Container | What's in it | Checked by |
|---|---|---|
| Pit bags | Hand tools, drivers, zip ties, tape | [Lead] Pit Lead |
| Competition box | Electrical spares, connectors, fuses | [Lead] Electrical Lead |
| Spares bin | Robot spare parts, belts, wheels | [Lead] Mechanical Lead |
| Spirit cart | Banners, spirit items, giveaways | [Lead] Spirit Lead |
| Pod Squad chest | Parts for helping other teams (RDOS-SOP-COMP-006) | [Lead] Pod Squad Lead |

## After the event
1. Unpack everything the next shop day.
2. Restock the pit bags and competition box; order what ran out.
3. Charge and test batteries.
