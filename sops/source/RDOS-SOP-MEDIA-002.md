## At a glance
1. **Request:** in #website (see Requesting a change)
2. **Prepare:** text and images (see Requesting a change)
3. **Edit:** WordPress (see Making the change)
4. **Check:** phone and desktop (see Making the change)

## Requesting a change
1. Post the request in #website with the page, the exact new text and any images.
2. Mentor bios need the mentor's approved photo and words.

## Making the change
1. Make the change in WordPress following the site's existing styles.
2. Check it on a phone and a desktop.
3. Reply in the thread when it's live.
