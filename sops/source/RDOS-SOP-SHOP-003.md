## At a glance
1. **Sort:** loose hardware into labeled bins (see Sorting hardware)
2. **Split:** new electronics between drawer and comp box (see Receiving new parts)
3. **Protect:** small screws in a magnetic dish (see Working rules)
4. **Count:** mentor cabinet inventory each term (see Mentor cabinet)
5. **Reorder:** low stock goes to purchasing (see Reordering)

## Purpose and scope
Lost hardware costs build time and money. This SOP covers fastener storage, electronics storage, the competition box and the mentor cabinet.

## Sorting hardware
1. Sort by type first: socket head, button head, flat head (countersunk), nuts, washers, spacers, wood and sheet metal screws, rivets.
2. Then sort by thread and length (for example 10-32 × 1/2 in.). Black oxide and stainless stay in separate bins.
3. Label every bin with type, thread size, length and finish.
4. Hardware from a robot being taken apart gets sorted the same night, not dumped in a mixed bin.

## Receiving new parts
1. When an order arrives, check it against the purchase request (RDOS-SOP-BUILD-003).
2. Electrical consumables: put about one third of each item in the competition box and the rest in the electrical drawer. [Team rule]
3. Comp-bot-only parts are labeled "COMP BOT ONLY" and stored with the competition robot's spares.

## Working rules
- Put small screws in a magnetic dish while you work. Kraken motors ship with exactly one set of cap and tensioning screws; every lost screw comes out of a small spare supply. [Team rule]
- Never take the last of anything without telling the Operations Lead.
- Unknown parts go in the "to identify" bin, not back in a random drawer.

## Mentor cabinet
High-value electronics (motors, controllers, sensors, radios) live in the mentor cabinet. [Mentor]
1. Count the cabinet at the start of each term and before each competition.
2. Record counts by item: motors by model, roboRIO or SystemCore units, PDP or PDH, breakers, CANcoders, CANivores, gyros, sensors, radios.
3. Sign out any item taken for a robot in [EDIT: where items are signed out].
