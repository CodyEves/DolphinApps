02Rules

  1. Don't remove tools from this kit for use during competition. Team rule
  2. The kit may be used to help an event with field assembly.
  3. Tell the Operations Lead before the event if anything is missing so it can be bought.

03Using the kit

  1. The kit is ready at load-in on the first day and especially on the last day for teardown.
  2. Logistics checks every tool back in at the end of teardown.
