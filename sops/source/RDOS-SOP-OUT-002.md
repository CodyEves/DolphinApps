summary: Demo robots (such as the minibot and Siren) must be working before every outreach event. Test them at least two shop days before.

## At a glance
1. **Schedule:** know the next event date (see Readiness check)
2. **Test:** two shop days before (see Readiness check)
3. **Fix:** claim open issues (see Fixing problems)
4. **Pack:** with the outreach list (see Readiness check)

## Readiness check
Run this two shop days before each event.
- [ ] Robot powers on and connects to the Driver Station
- [ ] Drives, turns and all modules calibrated
- [ ] Mechanisms work and are safe for crowds
- [ ] Robot signal light (RSL) works
- [ ] Bumpers and covers on
- [ ] Batteries charged and tested
- [ ] Radio set up; password held by a mentor (not in Slack)

## Fixing problems
1. Post the problem and the event date in #electrical or the robot's channel and ask for a volunteer.
2. Whoever takes the task replies in the thread and posts updates.
