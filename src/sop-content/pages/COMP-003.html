Match Queuing Notifications

Connect the event's queuing system (Nexus) to the team's match-queuing channel so the drive team is notified when to queue for each match.

01Setting up

  1. Before the first match, add the Nexus integration to #5199-match-queuing or #9271-match-queuing.
  2. Scan the event's Nexus code and choose the channel for notifications.
  3. Confirm the channel posts "This channel will receive queuing notifications".

02During the event

  1. "Now queuing" means go now. Move the robot to queue with the drive team.
  2. "On deck" means stay ready; the robot may already be queued.
  3. For playoffs and finals, queue as soon as possible and watch the official field timer. Notifications may stop.
  4. Need control system help? Scan the Nexus code and tap "Request technical help".

03After the event

  1. Turn off notifications for the finished event.
