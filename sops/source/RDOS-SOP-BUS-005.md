## At a glance
1. **Gate 1:** vendor and sponsor (see Gate 1: Vendor)
2. **Gate 2:** design (see Gate 2: Design)
3. **Gate 3:** pre-sale (see Gate 3: Pre-sale)
4. **Gate 4:** order and deliver (see Gate 4: Order and deliver)

## Merch gates
### Gate 1: Vendor
1. Apply for apparel sponsorships early in the fall (for example FRCTees).
2. Confirm shipping terms, minimums and lead times. Screen printing takes about two weeks; direct-to-film can be faster; rush orders cost extra.

### Gate 2: Design
1. The business team drafts designs with input from both teams.
2. Keep artwork in the same locations across garment types so they can share a price break.
3. Send art files to the vendor for a mockup by [EDIT: early January].

### Gate 3: Pre-sale
1. Open a pre-sale store so families order whenever they like.
2. Bulk order once or twice a year.

### Gate 4: Order and deliver
1. Place the order by [EDIT: date, e.g. mid-February] so it arrives before the first event.
2. Hand out orders and record who received what.
