Metal Lathe

Lathe work is done by signed-off operators with a Lead present. Remove the chuck key every time, keep sleeves and hair clear, and stop the spindle before measuring.

01Safety essentials

Never

  • Leave the chuck key in the chuck
  • Wear gloves, rings or loose sleeves
  • Measure or file a spinning part by hand without mentor training
  • Clear stringy chips with your hand
DANGER

Entanglement. A lathe can pull in a sleeve, glove or hair in less than a second. Tie hair back and stand out of line with the chuck when starting.

04Operation

  1. Mount the stock with the least stick-out that leaves room for the cut; tighten all jaws.
  2. Remove the chuck key and put it in its holder.
  3. Set spindle speed for the material and diameter using speed chart location.
  4. Start the spindle and take light passes. A common team job is rounding the ends of axles to fit bearings.
  5. Stop the spindle completely before measuring.

05Shutdown and cleanup

  1. Spindle off; remove the part and tools.
  2. Brush chips off the ways and into the tray. Use a chip hook for stringy chips.
  3. Oil the ways per maintenance schedule.

06Training and sign-off

  1. Read this SOP; pass the lathe safety test
  2. Pre-use check unprompted
  3. Supervised facing and turning to a dimension
  4. E-stop drill
