summary: Capture good photos, cull them before upload, file them by event, and publish only through the approval tool. Nothing goes straight to Instagram.

## At a glance
1. **Capture:** quality over quantity (see Capturing)
2. **Cull:** delete bad shots first (see Uploading)
3. **Upload:** event folder on the shared drive (see Uploading)
4. **Draft:** post in the scheduling tool (see Publishing)
5. **Approve:** mentor approval (see Publishing)
6. **Publish:** on schedule (see Publishing)

## Capturing
1. Capture team members, the robot, team numbers and merch, and moments that show who we are.
2. At outreach events, avoid kids' faces. Shoot from behind. Each photo should include at least one of: our team members, our team number or merch, or one of our robots. [Team rule]
3. Check the do-not-photograph list before posting anyone.
4. Don't touch the media team's cameras or equipment, even when unattended.

## Uploading
1. Delete blurry, accidental and duplicate shots before uploading. Don't upload hundreds of photos.
2. Don't edit before uploading; the media team edits only what gets used.
3. Upload to the shared media drive in the season folder, then the event folder. Create a clearly named folder if one is missing.
4. Photos of partner teams go in a subfolder named for that team so they can be shared with them.

## Publishing
1. KEY: Never post directly to Instagram or other team accounts. Drafts go into the social media scheduling tool (currently Buffer) or to the Media Lead. [Team rule]
2. A mentor approves the draft. [Mentor]
3. Posts go out on schedule with the right tags and branding.

## Content ideas
Pitch ideas to the Media Lead. A good idea fits at least two of these:
- **Brand:** shows our image and values
- **Subteam:** shows how subteams work together
- **Trend:** reaches beyond our supporters
- **Announcement:** news, events or achievements

## Taking part
Members are expected to take part in team photos and videos unless they have a task to finish. Everyone signed a photo release; check the do-not-photograph list for anyone who opted out.
